Services

Fast on mobile, where customers are

Most visitors come from mobile. A slow page loses them.

A page that loads slowly costs visitors and Google rankings. Often the cause is large images, heavy themes and needless scripts. I review the site, measure and fix what makes the biggest difference.

Common fixes

  • Images in the right size and modern formats
  • Smaller and fewer scripts
  • Fonts and icons loaded locally
  • A clear layout on small screens

Good to know

  • We measure before and after, so you see the difference
  • Faster pages help both visitors and Google
  • Fixes are made in steps, without the site going down

Frequently asked questions

Do we have to rebuild the whole site?

Rarely. Fixing images, scripts and a few templates is often enough.

How do you measure speed?

With the same kind of measurement Google uses, on both mobile and desktop.
