Services

Payments that land in the right place

The money should come in, and the books should know where from.

Payment is the last step for the customer and the first for finance. I connect the payment provider to orders and bookkeeping, so every payment can be traced from checkout to account.

What it can include

  • Card payments and other methods via a payment provider
  • Receipts and order confirmations by email
  • Refunds linked to returns
  • Bookkeeping records per day or per order

Relevant experience

  • Payments by card, PayPal and Amazon Payments (USA)
  • Mobile payments via MTN Mobile Money (SokoPay, Ghana)
  • ACCA qualification in accounting

Frequently asked questions

Which payment methods can we offer?

Those your payment provider supports. We choose the provider by fees and what your customers want to use.

Do you handle card details?

No. Card details are always handled by the payment provider, never by the website.
