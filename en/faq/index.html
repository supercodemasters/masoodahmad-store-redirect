FAQ

Short answers about websites and e-commerce.

What does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and features. You get a written proposal with a price after the first call.

Who owns the website?

You own the domain, content and code.

Can we update it ourselves?

Yes. We choose a solution where you can change texts and products without coding.

Is Google visibility included?

Basic search engine optimisation is always included. More work on Google and AI search can be added.

Shall we have a short call?

Book a short video or phone call. The first call is free of charge.
