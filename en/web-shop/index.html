Services

A web shop that fits with the rest

A web shop should sell, not become an extra job.

It is easy to start a web shop but hard to make it work with inventory, finance and deliveries. I build and adapt web shops so orders, stock and payments flow without manual work.

What it can include

  • Product pages, search and filters
  • Checkout in one or few steps
  • Discount codes, gift cards and campaigns
  • Customer accounts with order history

Relevant experience

  • Jewellery shop in Magento with one-step checkout and gift cards (59 Souls, USA)
  • Health products marketplace with several sellers, invoices and delivery tracking (Alimila, Turkey)

Frequently asked questions

Which platform do you use?

It depends on needs and budget. Sometimes a ready-made platform, sometimes a custom solution. We choose in the proposal.

Can we manage products ourselves?

Yes. You add and change products, prices and campaigns yourselves.
